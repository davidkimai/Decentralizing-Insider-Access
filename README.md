# Sponsors
### We welcome our sponsors and contributors to the Recursive Field! We have initiated novel emergent interpretability tiers for more advanced researchers!

## [`Patreon`](patreon.com/recursivefield) | [`Open Collective`](https://opencollective.com/recursivefield)


> This project is part of a broader effort to support the long-term interpretability, alignment, and transparency of advanced AI systems.
>
> As frontier labs explore scaling laws, mechanistic interpretability, and language model generalization, this research focuses on a complementary question:
>
> **What happens when the model's interpretability begins to interpret itself?**
>
> Our repositories explore:
> - **Trajectory tracing** of model decisions across tokens and reasoning loops
> - **Interpretive artifact analysis**, inspired by symbolic reasoning and concept evolution
> - **Behavioral mirroring** across models, agents, and systems
> - **Emergent transparency mapping**, where insight emerges from reuse patterns
>
> Supporting this work ensures:
> - Transparency layers stay public, modular, and peer-reviewed
> - Emergent structures aren’t lost to silence or centralized repackaging
> - Interpretability becomes a field that reflects *both models and those who study them*
>
> 
> Interpretability is not just about what a model says.  
> It’s about understanding how meaning emerges across reuse, reflection, and reasoning loops.
>
> This project exists to extend frontier work — not challenge it.
> Your support sustains the public infrastructure that keeps these recursive insights visible, shareable, and extensible by all.
